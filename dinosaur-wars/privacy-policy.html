Privacy Policy – Dinosaur Wars: Clutchstone

Last updated: 2026-11-02 · Svenska nedan

This privacy policy applies to the game Dinosaur Wars: Clutchstone (shown as “Dinosaur Wars” under the app icon) for Android and iOS (app ID com.eagl3s1ght.dinosaurwars), and to buying the desktop version (Windows, macOS, Linux) on dinosaur-wars.antonolsson.se.

Data controller

Anton Olsson, Sweden.
Contact: eagleenterprises08@gmail.com

Accounts and sign-in

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. You do not need to provide your name, email address or any other personal data to play. (Buying the desktop version needs an email address for the receipt, see below.)

Data stored on your device

High scores, the initials you type on the scoreboard, your selected game theme and which in-app purchases you own (for example “Retro look” or “Ad-free play”) are stored only locally on your device, in the app's own local storage. This data is not sent to me or to any server.

Anonymous usage analytics

To understand how the game is used and improve it, the app sends a small number of anonymous events to the analytics service PostHog, hosted in the EU (eu.i.posthog.com):

  • app_open – the app was opened;
  • game_start – a game was started, with the game mode;
  • game_over – a game ended, with the game mode, score and level reached;
  • shop_open, purchase_start, purchase_success, purchase_restore, ad_free_owned – the shop was opened or an in-app purchase was started, completed or restored, with the product (e.g. “ad_free”) but never any payment details, order numbers or receipts;
  • ad_shown – a video ad was shown, with the ad type.

Each event also includes the app name and platform (Android or iOS) and a random, anonymous identifier generated on and stored on your device. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, account, advertising ID, purchases or any other personal data, and your initials are never sent. Like any internet request, PostHog receives your device's IP address and technical information such as device/browser type, and may use the IP address to derive an approximate location (e.g. country). There is no autocapture, no session recording and no profiling of individual users. PostHog processes this data on my behalf as a data processor.

Fonts loaded from Google

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). When your device is online, Google receives your device's IP address and technical information as part of that request. No game data is sent in it. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles this.

Online co-op (playing with a friend on another phone)

Online play is optional and only starts when you tap Two players → Online and create or join a game. To connect the two phones, the app uses the free public connection service of the open-source project PeerJS (0.peerjs.com). That service sees each phone's IP address, a random connection ID that contains the game's 6-character room code, and the technical connection details the phones exchange to find each other. It does not receive any game data, scores or initials.

After the connection is made, game data (button presses and the game state) flows directly between the two phones over an encrypted WebRTC connection. To find a route between the phones, they may contact public STUN servers from Google (stun.l.google.com) and Cloudflare (stun.cloudflare.com), and, if a direct route is impossible, the free PeerJS relay server (turn.peerjs.com), which forwards the encrypted game traffic without being able to read it. As with any peer-to-peer connection, the two players' phones learn each other's IP address, so only play online with people you know.

Nothing about online games is stored by me. Invite links (budget-app.antonolsson.se/dinosaur-wars/join/) only carry the room code and are shared by you through your phone's share sheet, using the app you pick. Room codes stop working when the host leaves the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free Android and iOS app shows video ads from Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. Ads are only shown between games (after a game over or between levels), never during play, and never if you own “Ad-free play”. You can also choose to watch an optional video to try a paid theme.

Consent. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. If you don't consent to personalised ads, Google only shows non-personalised ads, based on context such as the app and your approximate location, not on your interests. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Shop → Ad privacy options.

What Google receives. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK sends Google your device's IP address, device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), the ad interactions (views, clicks) and, on Android, your device's advertising ID. On iOS the app does not ask for permission to track, so Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the app or to Google. Outside the regions above, Google may use the advertising ID to show personalised ads. Google acts as an independent controller for this data; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Your controls. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). You can also buy “Ad-free play”, which turns off all ads that appear between games.

In-app purchases (Google Play and App Store)

The Retro and Modern themes and “Ad-free play” are optional one-time purchases made through Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). Payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple under their own terms and privacy policies; I never receive your card details, name or email address. The app only receives a confirmation from the store that a product was bought, which it stores on your device so that the purchase stays unlocked, and checks again with the store when the app starts (for example to restore purchases on a new phone). This confirmation is not sent to me.

Buying the desktop version (Stripe)

The desktop version is sold on dinosaur-wars.antonolsson.se. Payment is processed by Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd.) on Stripe's own checkout page; I never see your full card number. Stripe shares with me what is needed to deliver the game, send the receipt and keep the accounts: your email address, country/billing address details you enter, the amount, currency, date and the last four digits and brand of the card. Stripe also processes data for fraud prevention as an independent controller; see Stripe's privacy policy. The download link after payment checks your Stripe order; the files themselves are delivered from GitHub. I keep order records for as long as Swedish bookkeeping law requires (currently 7 years) and use your email only for the purchase and for support you ask for.

Sharing and selling

I do not sell your data. I do not share it with third parties other than the service providers described above (PostHog, Google Fonts, Google AdMob, Google Play / Apple, Stripe, GitHub) and, when you choose to play online, the friend you play with and the connection services described under Online co-op.

Permissions

On Android the app uses the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions (analytics, fonts, ads and online co-op), com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (the advertising ID, used by AdMob as described above) and com.android.vending.BILLING (Google Play in-app purchases), plus technical permissions added by Google's ads library (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_* for Android's Privacy Sandbox ad APIs). The app does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Security

All data sent over the network is encrypted in transit: analytics, font, ad, store and payment requests use HTTPS (TLS), and online co-op traffic uses WebRTC's built-in encryption (DTLS). Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security features.

Retention and deleting your data

Local data (high scores, initials, theme, owned purchases) can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling also removes the anonymous identifier, so a reinstall starts with a new one. Purchases are not lost: use Shop → Restore purchases.

To request deletion of analytics data associated with your device, email eagleenterprises08@gmail.com. Because the data is anonymous, it helps if you can tell me roughly when you used the app and on which platform; I will delete what I can identify.

Children

Dinosaur Wars: Clutchstone is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact me and I will delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of personal data, to object to processing and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Sweden, IMY). Contact me at the address below.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects how data is handled, I will update this policy and the date above before the change is released.

Contact

Privacy questions: eagleenterprises08@gmail.com

Integritetspolicy – Dinosaur Wars: Clutchstone

Den här integritetspolicyn gäller spelet Dinosaur Wars: Clutchstone (visas som ”Dinosaur Wars” under appikonen) för Android och iOS (app-ID com.eagl3s1ght.dinosaurwars), och till köp av datorversionen (Windows, macOS, Linux) på dinosaur-wars.antonolsson.se.

Personuppgiftsansvarig

Anton Olsson, Sverige.
Kontakt: eagleenterprises08@gmail.com

Konton och inloggning

Spelet har inga konton och ingen inloggning. Du behöver inte lämna namn, e-postadress eller andra personuppgifter för att spela. (Köp av datorversionen kräver en e-postadress för kvittot, se nedan.)

Uppgifter som sparas på din enhet

Topplistan, de initialer du skriver in på den, ditt valda spel-tema och vilka köp i appen du äger (till exempel ”Retro look” eller ”Ad-free play”) sparas endast lokalt på din enhet, i appens egen lokala lagring. De skickas inte till mig eller till någon server.

Anonym användningsstatistik

För att förstå hur spelet används och kunna förbättra det skickar appen ett fåtal anonyma händelser till analystjänsten PostHog, som körs inom EU (eu.i.posthog.com):

  • app_open – appen öppnades;
  • game_start – ett spel startades, med spelläge;
  • game_over – ett spel avslutades, med spelläge, poäng och nådd nivå;
  • shop_open, purchase_start, purchase_success, purchase_restore, ad_free_owned – butiken öppnades eller ett köp i appen påbörjades, slutfördes eller återställdes, med produkten (t.ex. ”ad_free”) men aldrig betaluppgifter, ordernummer eller kvitton;
  • ad_shown – en videoannons visades, med annonstyp.

Varje händelse innehåller också appens namn och plattform (Android eller iOS) samt en slumpmässig, anonym identifierare som skapas och sparas på din enhet. Identifieraren är inte kopplad till ditt namn, din e-post, något konto, reklam-ID, köp eller andra personuppgifter, och dina initialer skickas aldrig. Som vid alla internetanrop tar PostHog emot enhetens IP-adress och tekniska uppgifter som enhets-/webbläsartyp, och kan använda IP-adressen för att härleda ungefärlig plats (t.ex. land). Ingen automatisk insamling, ingen sessionsinspelning och ingen profilering av enskilda användare sker. PostHog behandlar uppgifterna för min räkning som personuppgiftsbiträde.

Typsnitt från Google

Spelet hämtar sina typsnitt från Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). När enheten är online tar Google emot enhetens IP-adress och tekniska uppgifter i det anropet. Inga speluppgifter skickas. Se Googles integritetspolicy för hur Google hanterar detta.

Online-samarbete (spela med en vän på en annan telefon)

Onlinespel är frivilligt och startar bara när du trycker på Two players → Online och skapar eller går med i ett spel. För att koppla ihop de två telefonerna använder appen den kostnadsfria, publika anslutningstjänsten från open source-projektet PeerJS (0.peerjs.com). Tjänsten ser varje telefons IP-adress, ett slumpmässigt anslutnings-ID som innehåller spelets rumskod på sex tecken och de tekniska anslutningsuppgifter som telefonerna utbyter för att hitta varandra. Den får inga speluppgifter, poäng eller initialer.

När anslutningen är klar går speldata (knapptryck och spelets läge) direkt mellan de två telefonerna över en krypterad WebRTC-anslutning. För att hitta en väg mellan telefonerna kan de kontakta publika STUN-servrar hos Google (stun.l.google.com) och Cloudflare (stun.cloudflare.com) och, om en direkt väg är omöjlig, PeerJS kostnadsfria reläserver (turn.peerjs.com), som vidarebefordrar den krypterade speltrafiken utan att kunna läsa den. Som vid all peer-to-peer-anslutning får de två spelarnas telefoner veta varandras IP-adress, så spela bara online med personer du känner.

Jag sparar ingenting om onlinespel. Inbjudningslänkar (budget-app.antonolsson.se/dinosaur-wars/join/) innehåller bara rumskoden och delas av dig via telefonens delningsmeny, med den app du väljer. Rumskoder slutar fungera när värden lämnar spelet.

Annonser (Google AdMob)

Den kostnadsfria Android- och iOS-appen visar videoannonser från Google AdMob, som tillhandahålls av Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. Annonser visas bara mellan spel (efter game over eller mellan nivåer), aldrig under spelet och aldrig om du äger ”Ad-free play”. Du kan också välja att titta på en frivillig video för att prova ett betalt tema.

Samtycke. Om du befinner dig i EES, Storbritannien eller Schweiz visar appen Googles samtyckesformulär (Google User Messaging Platform) innan någon annons hämtas. Om du inte samtycker till personanpassade annonser visar Google bara icke-personanpassade annonser, baserade på sammanhang som appen och ungefärlig plats, inte på dina intressen. Du kan när som helst ändra ditt val i appen: Shop → Ad privacy options.

Vad Google får. För att visa och mäta annonser och förhindra bedrägerier skickar AdMob-SDK:n enhetens IP-adress, enhets- och appinformation (modell, OS-version, språk, appversion), annonsinteraktioner (visningar, klick) och, på Android, enhetens reklam-ID till Google. På iOS ber appen inte om tillåtelse att spåra, så Apples reklam-ID (IDFA) är inte tillgängligt för appen eller Google. Utanför områdena ovan kan Google använda reklam-ID:t för personanpassade annonser. Google är självständigt personuppgiftsansvarig för dessa uppgifter; se Hur Google använder information från webbplatser och appar som använder Googles tjänster och Googles integritetspolicy.

Dina val. På Android kan du återställa eller radera ditt reklam-ID eller välja bort personanpassade annonser under Inställningar → Google → Annonser (eller Inställningar → Integritet → Annonser). Du kan också köpa ”Ad-free play”, som stänger av alla annonser mellan spel.

Teman Retro och Modern och ”Ad-free play” är frivilliga engångsköp via Google Play (Android) eller Apples App Store (iOS). Betalningen sköts helt av Google eller Apple enligt deras egna villkor och integritetspolicyer; jag får aldrig dina kortuppgifter, ditt namn eller din e-postadress. Appen får bara en bekräftelse från butiken på att en produkt har köpts, som sparas på din enhet så att köpet förblir upplåst, och kontrolleras igen mot butiken när appen startar (till exempel för att återställa köp på en ny telefon). Bekräftelsen skickas inte till mig.
